Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

NBA Stables is a free stats site. We do not require user accounts.

What We Process

  • Server logs with the requested page, response status and response time. No IP address or other visitor identifier is logged.
  • Local browser storage for your settings (selected league, pinned stats). It stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

Why We Process Data

  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Count page and feature usage from those logs to improve performance and content.

Legal Basis

Where applicable (EEA/UK), we rely on legitimate interests for operation and security. We use no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools.

Retention

Logs are retained only as long as reasonably needed for operations, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Your Rights

We store no IP address, name, account or other data that identifies you, so there is nothing of yours to access, correct or erase. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Croatia this is AZOP (azop.hr).

Hosting

The site runs on servers rented from Hetzner Online GmbH, which stores server logs on our behalf. Fonts and scripts are served from our own server; no data is sent to Google or other font or script providers.

Third-Party Sources

NBA Stables displays data from third-party sports data sources, which may be delayed or incomplete.
