About NBA Stables

NBA Stables is a free web app for live NBA and WNBA stats. No ads, no paywalls, no tracking.

Features

🏀

Live Scoreboard

Real-time scores, game clock and team leaders for every NBA and WNBA game. Updates itself while games are on.

📊

Box Scores

Team and player stats with quarter scores, +/-, starters and inactive players for today's games and the past week.

🔍

Player Tracker

Look up any player and follow their live stats across games. Updates itself while games are on.

🏆

Daily Leaders

Who led in points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and 3-pointers, today or any day of the past week.

📈

Standings

Standings with home and away records, streaks, last 10 games and points per game for both leagues.

🩹

Injury Reports

Current NBA injuries from CBS Sports.

🏅

Playoffs

Playoff bracket with series scores, the play-in tournament and the Finals.

🥇

Season Leaders

Top 10 per-game averages in points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, 3-pointers, FG% and FT%.

⭐

Season Highs

Best single-game performances of the season in every category.

🎯

Doubles & Triples

Season double-double and triple-double counts, with every triple-double game.

👤

Player Profile

Bio, last 10 games, season averages and career stats for any player.

🔄

Trades

NBA trades, signings and waivers from the past six months.

Why this exists

Most sports sites are slow and full of ads. NBA Stables covers both NBA and WNBA in one app that works on any device. Install it to your home screen if you want.

Always free

Free, no account needed, no cookies.
